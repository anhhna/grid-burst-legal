Support

Questions, bug reports or purchase problems: support@infinyt.app. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Restoring purchases

Open Settings → Restore purchases in the game while signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

Cancelling Grid Burst Plus

Open Settings → Manage subscription in the game, or manage it in your App Store / Google Play account settings. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
