Privacy Policy

Grid Burst – Block Puzzle · Last updated November 21, 2026

This policy explains what information the Grid Burst mobile game (“the App”), published by Infinyt (“we”, “us”), handles and why. We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address or contacts.

1. Information stored on your device

Your game progress, scores, coins, power-ups, settings and purchased themes are saved locally on your device. This data is not sent to us. Deleting the App deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To serve, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • device and advertising identifiers (Apple IDFA if you allow tracking, Google Advertising ID on Android);
  • IP address, approximate location derived from it, device model, operating system and language;
  • ad interactions (impressions, clicks, rewarded video completions) and crash diagnostics of the ad SDK.

On iOS the App asks for permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt; if you decline, the IDFA is not shared and you receive non-personalised ads. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland the App shows Google’s consent form before personalised ads are loaded, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

3. Purchases (Apple, Google and RevenueCat)

Payments are processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play; we never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to check which purchases and subscriptions are active on your device. RevenueCat receives a random anonymous app user ID, your purchase receipts and transaction history, and basic device information (OS version, country, App version). See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

4. How we use information

  • to show ads that keep the App free, and to stop showing them if you purchase Remove Ads or Grid Burst Plus;
  • to unlock and restore your purchases;
  • to keep the App working and secure.

We do not sell personal information and we do not use it for any purpose other than those listed here.

5. Retention

Local game data stays on your device until you delete the App. Data held by Google and RevenueCat is kept according to their policies linked above.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold personal data ourselves, most requests are handled directly by Google or RevenueCat; you can also reset your advertising ID in your device settings. Contact us and we will help with any request.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The “last updated” date above shows when it last changed.
